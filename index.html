Field Manual // FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need to own Star Wars Battlefront II (2005)?

Yes — this is a Bring-Your-Own-Copy browser engine. Point the portal at your legal Steam, GOG, or Disc GameData folder; your files remain 100% on your device and are never uploaded.

Can friends join a match already in progress?

Yes. Live matches remain listed as In Match · Joinable in the lobby browser, and sharing your Invite Link lets friends hot-join straight onto the battlefield.

How do text chat, voice chat, and fullscreen pause work?

Press T for All Chat, Y for Team Chat, and hold V for Push-to-Talk voice. Tapping Esc pauses the game in fullscreen so you can respawn or change teams cleanly.

Are Space Battles, XL Mode, and Save Imports included?

Yes — all 24 maps (including Hoth XL, Geonosis XL, Kashyyyk XL, Hero Assault, Hunt, CTF, and all 6 Space Assault arenas) work solo or online, with full save export & import.
